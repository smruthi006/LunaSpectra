# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

As contributors and maintainers of **LunaSpectra**, we are committed to fostering an open, respectful, inclusive, and academically rigorous collaborative environment for all team members, faculty reviewers, and external contributors regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate constructively, clearly, and respectfully in issues, pull requests, code reviews, and team meetings.
- Focus feedback on technical merit, reproducibility, and scientific accuracy rather than personal critique.
- Acknowledge uncertainties, limitations, and honest mistakes transparently.
- Respect academic integrity, proper attribution, and data-usage terms (including ISRO/ISSDC and lunar imagery guidelines).

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, demeaning remarks, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate fabrication of scientific claims, metrics, test outputs, or dataset provenance.
- Plagiarism or unattributed copying of external code, figures, or documentation.
- Publishing private contact details, credentials, or restricted materials without permission.
